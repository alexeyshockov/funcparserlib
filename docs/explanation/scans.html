How a scan walks a text

Parser.scan_text() tries a grammar at one offset at a time. Pick a grammar, edit the text, and step. The prose, and the source of truth, is How a Scan Walks a Text.

Grammar and text

The grammars read text without a tokenizer, so every offset is tried. A grammar of tokenized() is tried only where a token starts.

The text

Grey: offsets tried without a match. Green: matches. Underlined: the next offset to try.
